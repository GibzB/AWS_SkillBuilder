# Show when a day ends for streak badges, and when progress updates

I want to know exactly when a day starts and ends for the visit, like and comment streak badges. Today the badge popup shows progress such as 20 of 30 days, but not whether a day follows my local time or UTC, or when the count updates.

The feature would allow me to:
- See the time zone and the cut-off time for a streak day on the badge page
- See when the counters were last updated
- Tell whether an action done late in the evening counts for today or tomorrow

This would help builders in time zones far from UTC, who cannot tell whether a streak is safe or already broken. It would also remove guesswork for anyone keeping a 30 or 90 day streak.
